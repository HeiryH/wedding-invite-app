Output: `frontend/public/templates/painted-promise/assets/welcome-ring-v4/ring-visible-front-angle.png`.

EXACT VISIBLE-PIXEL EXTRACTION, not a new jewelry rendering.
From the attached bride image isolate ONLY the visible small gold gemstone ring at x about 19.3%, y about 70%. Preserve its EXACT on-hand viewing angle, screen rotation, foreshortening, silhouette, round pale gemstone, gold scroll setting, color and soft shading.
Remove the hand and all lace/background to genuine transparency, leaving ONLY the visible FRONT ring segment. The output should look like the same curved cap/arch of jewelry lifted straight off the finger, gemstone above and two short gold shoulders descending to either side. DO NOT complete the hidden back half of the band. DO NOT create a complete oval hoop or large circular opening. Hidden portions stay absent/transparent. Do not tilt, rotate, mirror, straighten or reorient the ring to display it as a catalog product.
You may uniformly enlarge the exact visible ring segment for usable resolution and center it with transparent margins. Maintain its precise relative geometry: gemstone near the upper center, left shoulder extending left and slightly down, right shoulder curving farther down on the right, exactly as shown on the finger. All skin becomes transparent even within/beneath this arch.
One ring fragment only, no hand/skin/lace, no rear band, no invented underside, no shadow/glow, no text, no background. Original painted style and palette unchanged.
