# 05 — Wishes: Leave Something With Us

Apply `../style.md` and the continuity rules in `../scene.md` exactly.

## Story beat

The camera glides along the same reception table and arrives at an open wedding guest book. A ribbon
bookmark leaves the book and rises toward the memory wall.

## Full-design prompt

Continue the exact RSVP tabletop height, direction, folds and lighting. Centre an open blank guest
book with ivory pages and a restrained embossed geometric border. An espresso fountain pen rests
across the lower page. A silk ribbon bookmark travels upward/right.

Around the book place a small empty ring dish, one pressed flower, loose petals, sealed wish
envelopes, a petite lantern and a narrow vase with airy stems. Maintain ample space for live Wishes
content. Do not generate faux handwriting.

## Clean-background edit prompt

Remove the book, pen, ribbon, dish, flower, petals, envelopes, lantern, vase, and all text/UI. Preserve
the table, linen, venue atmosphere and distant background.
