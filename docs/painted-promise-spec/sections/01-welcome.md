# 01 — Welcome: The Ring

Apply `../style.md` and the continuity rules in `../scene.md` exactly.

## Story beat

On load, the groom gently slides a ring onto the bride's ring finger. Only the hands and wrists are
visible. After the ring settles, a small painted shimmer appears and the names/date/countdown reveal.
Scrolling pulls the camera away from this close-up into the Walimah scene.

## Full-design prompt

Render one tall portrait e-invitation scene. Create an extreme close-up, slightly elevated
three-quarter view. The bride's left hand rests diagonally from lower centre toward upper right,
fingers elegant and relaxed, wearing a white bridal lace sleeve. The groom's single right hand enters
from the lower left in a dark formal cuff and slides a delicate antique-gold wedding ring onto her
ring finger. There is no second supporting hand. Anatomy and ring placement must be credible.

Behind the hands is an airy powder-blue painted atmosphere with warm ivory light. Loose ivory,
blush and sage florals enter from the outer corners. The lower and side edges hint at drapery and
floral forms that can survive the camera pullback into the reception venue.

Reserve a calm vertical corridor through the upper-centre and centre-right for live names, date,
opening copy and countdown. Representative text may appear in the full design only. No faces,
bodies, second groom hand, chairs, programme board, table, photo frames or fake controls.

## Clean-background edit prompt

Using the accepted full design, remove the bride's hand and sleeve, groom's hand and cuff, ring,
foreground floral clusters, shimmer marks, and all text/UI. Reconstruct the complete painted scene
behind them while preserving canvas, lighting, brushwork and camera.

## Prop-atlas prompt

Create a transparent atlas containing exactly:

1. bride's complete left hand and wrist with white bridal lace sleeve;
2. groom's complete right hand and wrist with dark formal cuff;
3. antique-gold ring by itself;
4. upper-left floral framing cluster;
5. upper-right floral framing cluster;
6. lower-edge floral/drapery transition cluster;
7. three small painted shimmer marks as one group.

No text, scene background, extra hands, jewellery, boxes or unrelated props.
