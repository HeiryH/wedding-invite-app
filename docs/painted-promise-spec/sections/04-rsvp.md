# 04 — RSVP: Your Place Awaits

Apply `../style.md` and the continuity rules in `../scene.md` exactly.

## Story beat

The camera moves beyond the programme and follows the aisle to a refined reception table. One empty
chair and prepared place setting wait for the guest.

## Full-design prompt

Render the same reception room and lighting. The aisle reaches an elegant table covered in matte
warm-ivory linen. One empty chair is drawn slightly back. At the place are an ivory plate, folded
napkin, clear glassware, blank place card, blank menu card and sealed blush envelope. Add a low airy
floral arrangement and small glass lantern/candle. Reserve a calm central area for live RSVP content.
The table continues laterally toward the guest-book station, with a fine ivory/blush ribbon providing
the handoff.

## Clean-background edit prompt

Remove the chair, place setting, cards, envelope, floral arrangement, lantern/candle, ribbon, and all
text/UI. Preserve the room, distant aisle, table body and uninterrupted ivory linen.

## Prop-atlas prompt

Create a transparent atlas containing exactly:

1. empty upholstered chair;

3. clear glassware group with no alcohol;
4. blank place card;
5. blank menu card;
6. sealed blush envelope;
7. low ivory/blush/sage floral arrangement;
8. glass lantern with candle;
9. ribbon transition accent;
10. small painted floral confirmation mark.
