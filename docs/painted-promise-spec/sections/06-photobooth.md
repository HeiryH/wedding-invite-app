# 06 — Photobooth: The Memory Wall

Apply `../style.md` and the continuity rules in `../scene.md` exactly.

## Story beat

The camera follows the guest-book ribbon upward into a suspended ribbon-and-photo installation framed
by florals and drapery. Real guest photos populate the blank frames. The camera pulls back for a final
view with warm lantern light and a distant echo of the dais.

## Full-design prompt

Render the same reception venue. The long ivory/blush ribbon enters from the lower edge and joins a
light suspended installation of ribbons and small blank photo frames. Every opening is empty warm
ivory or pale blue for live guest images.

Frame it with a contemporary floral arch using the same ivory, blush, peach, sage and olive flowers
from the dais. Add sheer ivory drapery, decorative clips, a small illustrated camera, empty printed-
photo basket, warm lanterns and near foreground flowers. Through it, show a soft distant suggestion
of the original powder-blue dais arch.

## Clean-background edit prompt

Remove ribbons, frames, clips, floral-arch foreground pieces, camera, basket, movable lanterns, near
flowers, and all text/UI. Preserve the venue, distant drapery, ambient light and distant dais echo.

## Prop-atlas prompt

Create a transparent atlas containing exactly:

1. long ribbon system;
2. three blank portrait frames with clips;
3. three blank landscape/square frames with clips;
4. left floral-arch section;
5. right floral-arch section;
6. sheer drapery accent;
7. small illustrated camera;
8. empty photo basket;
9. left warm lantern cluster;
10. right warm lantern cluster;
11. near-camera floral branch;
12. drifting-petal group.
