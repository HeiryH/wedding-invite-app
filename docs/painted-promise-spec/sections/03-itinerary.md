# 03 — Itinerary: Follow the Celebration

Apply `../style.md` and the continuity rules in `../scene.md` exactly.

## Story beat

After returning to the complete dais, the camera follows the outgoing floral garland off the platform
and along the aisle to an elegant freestanding programme display. Schedule entries reveal in sequence.

## Full-design prompt

Render a tall portrait continuation of the dais venue. The upper edge retains a soft distant glimpse
of the dais and powder-blue arch. A continuous ivory/blush/sage floral garland descends from it and
guides the eye through the frame. A pale ivory aisle runner and antique-gold edge lights establish
depth.

The camera arrives at a slender freestanding programme board with a warm ivory face, fine
antique-gold frame and softly scalloped contemporary arch top. Keep its face blank for live programme
rows. Add two asymmetrical glass lanterns, protected candles, scattered petals, partial drapery and
small floral stems. The lower aisle continues toward the reception tables.

## Clean-background edit prompt

Remove the programme board, foreground flowers, movable lanterns, petals, and all text/UI. Preserve
the distant dais glimpse, aisle, fixed edge lighting, venue atmosphere and continuous garland portions.

## Prop-atlas prompt

Create a transparent atlas containing exactly:

1. blank freestanding programme board;
2. left board floral cluster;
3. right board floral cluster;
4. tall glass lantern;
5. short glass lantern with candle;
6. candle-vessel trio;
7. loose-petal trail;
8. near-camera floral stem;
9. short ivory drapery/ribbon accent.
