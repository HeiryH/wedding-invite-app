# 02 — Walimah and couple: The Dais

Apply `../style.md` and the continuity rules in `../scene.md` exactly.

## Story beat

The Welcome close-up pulls back into a full contemporary reception dais. The Walimah paragraph
reveals first. Continued scrolling establishes the seated couple, moves into a groom close-up,
travels across to a bride close-up, and returns to the complete dais.

## Full-design prompt

Render a tall portrait master of a contemporary Muslim wedding dais. The bride and groom sit side by
side in separate elegant upholstered chairs on a low ivory platform. The bride wears an ivory hijab,
modest long-sleeved gown and subtle lace cuff. The groom wears a dark espresso formal suit and ivory
shirt. Their poses are relaxed, affectionate and dignified.

Behind them is one layered powder-blue architectural arch softened by ivory drapery. Asymmetrical
ivory/blush florals and sage foliage grow around it. Use restrained antique-gold chair detail, glass
lanterns and warm candles. A floral garland leaves the lower right of the platform and travels toward
the foreground, becoming the path to Programme.

Reserve negative space above and between the couple for a Walimah title and paragraph, plus calm side
pockets for groom and bride details.

## Camera targets

## Clean-background edit prompt

Remove the bride, groom, both chairs, foreground lantern clusters, near-camera florals, loose petals,
and all text/UI. Preserve the platform, arch, drapery, fixed distant floral masses, ambient candles
and outgoing floral path. Reconstruct all hidden areas.
